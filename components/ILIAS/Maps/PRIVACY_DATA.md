## Data being presented

- If the person has published it in their profile, the affiliation (institution and department) and the postal address are presented in the marker of a person on the map to every person who can see the map, to show who is where.
- If the person has published their location in their profile, the location and the name are presented in the marker of a person on the map to every person who can see the map, to show who is where.
- The login is presented in the list of persons beside the map to every person who can see the map, to open the marker of a person on the map.
